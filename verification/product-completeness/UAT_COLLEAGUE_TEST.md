# Colleague UAT 1.0 — Merchant tasks

## Unscripted tasks

1. Sign in and identify the current workspace and subscription state.
2. Change your display name and save it.
3. Upload a valid avatar and then try an invalid file.
4. Find the business template selector and compare two templates.
5. Apply a template, inspect the result, undo it, then reapply and save.
6. Reorder bottom navigation with the keyboard.
7. Hide a navigation item and confirm the minimum visible count is enforced.
8. Mark one navigation item as featured and save.
9. Edit the home page and verify the preview becomes dirty.
10. Use Undo and Redo and describe what changed.
11. Generate the latest preview.
12. Open Scan Preview and inspect QR, timestamp and status.
13. Trigger a preview failure and explain the displayed reason.
14. Open Customer Center and search for an identified user.
15. Open Customer 360 and inspect appointments, membership, activity and notes.
16. Add a customer note.
17. Make a points adjustment and verify the balance and ledger effect.
18. Open Appointment settings and change a rule.
19. Open AI Customer Service, edit an FAQ and test a rules answer.
20. Configure/test BYOK without exposing the API key.

## Final questions

1. What data was saved and what still required an explicit Save?
2. Which source did the preview use?
3. Could you tell whether a customer was identified without seeing OpenID?
4. What happened when you tried an invalid avatar?
5. Which navigation constraints were enforced?
6. Did Undo/Redo match your expectation?
7. What did Preview status and timestamp communicate?
8. Could you find a real FAQ source for an answer?
9. Was any platform AI or fake metric visible?
10. What would you change before using this with a colleague?
